⚠️ 50 DEADLY TRAPS — Click to Reveal

These are the most common mistakes flagged by ACCA examiners. Click each one to reveal the answer.

1. PA taper when ANI > £100,000?
✓ YES — PA reduced by £1 per £2 above £100k. Zero PA at £125,140.
2. Full Expensing for sole traders?
✗ NO — Full Expensing (100% FYA) applies ONLY to companies. Sole traders use AIA.
3. Pension reduces Class 4 NIC?
✗ NO — Pension contributions extend IT bands but have NO effect on NIC.
4. AIA on motor cars?
✗ NO — Cars are EXCLUDED from AIA. They go into main/special rate pool only.
5. 4% diesel surcharge for RDE2?
✗ NO — RDE2-compliant diesels are EXEMPT from the 4% surcharge.
6. Business use reduces car benefit?
✗ NO — Car benefit is NOT reduced for business use. Private use % is irrelevant.
7. Dormant companies count as associated?
✗ NO — Dormant companies are EXCLUDED from the associated company count.
8. Apply AEA against BADR gains first?
✗ NO — Apply AEA against higher-rate gains (24%) first to maximise tax saving!
9. IHT taper relief reduces gift value?
✗ NO — Taper relief reduces the TAX PAYABLE, not the gift value!
10. 60-day CGT property deadline?
✓ YES — 60 days from completion for UK residential property CGT return AND payment.
